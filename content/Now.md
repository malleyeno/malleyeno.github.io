*This Now page is inspired by https://nownownow.com/about. The goal of a Now page is to talk about what is going on in my life now and what I am focusing on.*

This year, my theme is Cool. That might seem like a weird thing to theme a year around, but it didn’t come from nowhere. I’ve been feeling like my life as of late was pretty lame: I go to work, I come home, I chill out, I go to bed, and repeat. Living like that is a blessing I realize, I am not being threatened nor under attack. But seeing some of the interesting things that people around me are getting up to makes me feel like I’m just floating along in comparison.

So yeah, this year, I want to feel cool. I want to do cool things, wear cool clothes, and live in a way that makes other think “dang, that guy’s cool!”

So far, I think I’m doing okay on that front! I dress a lot cooler, at least in my opinion. I like to wear neutral earthy tones that are just blocks of colour — I feel like neutral blocks are making a comeback! It’s vintage~
But beyond feeling cool, I think I am doing cool things. I got a house (!!) before I turned 30. Villy and I are making big plans for both the house and for our futures. And there are a few projects I’m pursuing: I started making a live2d tuber (technically using Inochi2d, so not actually live2d but saying just 2d tuber is confusing). I think it’s coming out okay! This blog is getting a face lift and some TLC. I’d love to do more long form writing. 

Actually I was considering doing straight up videos, I have a list of ideas on that. But with how much of my time has been taken up as of late, I think I will stick with writing. And if I think I can get myself up to making videos, I can pull from those writings as scripts. Makes sense to me, at least for now.

That’s what my now looks like!
